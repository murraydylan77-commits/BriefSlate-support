# BriefSlate Support

BriefSlate is a calm Markdown notes app for iPhone and iPad. Your notes are plain `.md` files in a folder you choose, so they stay yours.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where are my notes stored?

By default, notes are stored on your device. You can see them in the Files app under **On My iPhone** (or **On My iPad**) → **BriefSlate**. Every note is a regular Markdown file you can open in any app.

### How do I sync notes between my iPhone and iPad?

Go to **Settings → Notes → Storage & Backup** and choose a folder in iCloud Drive. Choose the same folder on each device. Apple syncs the files through your iCloud account.

### iCloud has two versions of a note. What do I do?

If a note was edited on two devices before they synced, BriefSlate asks you to review it. You can keep this version, keep the other version, or keep both.

### I deleted a note by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes. Deleted notes go to the **Trash** in the sidebar, and you can restore them from there until you empty it. Earlier versions of a note may also be available under **Settings → Notes → Local Recovery Versions**.

### How do I back up my notes?

Go to **Settings → Notes → Storage & Backup** to back up your workspace to a folder you choose. You can also use **Settings → Notes → Export All Notes as ZIP** to save a copy of everything.

### How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Go to **Settings**, scroll to **Premium**, and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

### I forgot the passphrase for a protected note.

Protected notes are encrypted on your device, and your passphrase is never stored or sent anywhere. That means nobody, including the developer, can recover a protected note without it. Consider keeping your passphrase in a password manager.

### Why don't I see Summarize Note or Clean Up Writing?

These features use Apple Intelligence, which runs on your device. They appear only on devices that support Apple Intelligence, when it's turned on in the Settings app.

### How do I link notes together?

Type `[[` followed by a note's name to link to it. If you later rename or move that note, BriefSlate updates the links for you.
