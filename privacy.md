# BriefSlate Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 9, 2026

BriefSlate is a notes app for iPhone and iPad. It is designed so that your notes stay yours. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

## The short version

- BriefSlate does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.
- The developer does not operate any servers that receive your notes or usage data.

## Your notes and attachments

Your notes are stored as plain files in a folder you choose: on your device, in iCloud Drive, or in another location you've added to the Files app, such as a cloud storage app, a network drive or a USB drive. If you choose a folder in iCloud Drive or another provider, that provider stores and syncs the files under its own terms and privacy policy (for iCloud Drive, [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)). The developer cannot access them.

Photos and files you add to a note are saved alongside your notes in the same folder. Backups and exports you create are saved where you choose to put them. To help you recover from crashes and mistakes, BriefSlate also keeps recovery copies of recent changes in the app's own storage on your device; copies of protected notes stay encrypted.

## Protected notes

When you password-protect a note, it is encrypted on your device using your passphrase. Your passphrase is not stored or sent anywhere, so the developer cannot recover a protected note if you forget it.

## Photos

When you add a photo to a note, you choose it with the system photo picker, which gives BriefSlate access only to the photos you select. If you use **Save to Photos**, BriefSlate asks for permission to add images to your photo library. It does not read your library.

## Writing assistance

Writing features in BriefSlate use Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence) where it is available. The text you ask it to work with is processed on your device and is not sent to the developer.

## In-app purchases

BriefSlate Premium is sold through the Apple App Store. Apple processes the payment. The developer does not receive your name, payment details, or Apple ID. The app contacts the App Store only to show Premium's price, to make a purchase you start, and to confirm or restore Premium on your Apple account.

## Settings

Your app preferences, such as editor and display settings, are stored locally on your device.

## Children's privacy

BriefSlate does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
