# BriefSlate Privacy Policy  
  
**Effective date:** November 5, 2026  
  
## The short version  
  
BriefSlate does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer runs no servers that receive your notes or how you use the app.  
  
## Who can access your data  
  
Your notes, attachments and settings stay under your control. The developer has no access to them, and BriefSlate gives no third party (no company or person) access to them.  
  
BriefSlate has no servers, no accounts and no third-party code: no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools. The app has no built-in connection that sends your notes, attachments or any other content anywhere.  
  
Your data leaves your device only when you choose:  
- **iCloud Drive:** if you keep your notes folder in iCloud Drive, Apple syncs it under your iCloud account, as described in Apple's privacy policy. BriefSlate does not run or see that sync.  
- **Sharing and exporting:** a note or file goes only to the app or place you pick in the share sheet.  
- **Links:** tapping a web link in a note opens it in your browser.  
  
The only time BriefSlate itself contacts the internet is to ask Apple's App Store whether Premium has been purchased. That check never includes your notes.  
  
## Your notes and attachments  
  
Notes are plain files in a folder you choose, on your device or in iCloud Drive. Photos and files you add are stored next to your notes in that folder. Backups and exports are saved only where you put them.  
  
## Copies kept on your device  
  
- **Recovery copies:** BriefSlate keeps recent versions of your notes in its private storage on this device, so you can recover earlier text or unsaved changes. They are not included in device backups and are not synced. Recovery copies of protected notes are encrypted with that note's key.  
- **Temporary export files:** when you export or share, BriefSlate makes a temporary copy in its private temporary folder on your device so the share sheet can send it. The copy is deleted when sharing finishes, and any leftovers are cleaned up later.  
  
## Protected notes  
  
Password-protected notes are encrypted on your device with your passphrase. The passphrase is not stored or sent anywhere. If you forget it, the developer cannot recover the note.  
  
## Photos  
  
BriefSlate uses the system photo picker, so it can see only the photos you select. "Save to Photos" asks for permission to add images; it does not read your photo library.  
  
## Writing assistance  
  
Where available, writing features use Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence). Your text is processed on your device and is never sent to the developer.  
  
## In-app purchases  
  
Premium is sold through the App Store, and Apple processes the payment. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app checks with the App Store only to confirm whether Premium has been purchased.  
  
## Settings  
  
Your preferences, such as editor and display settings, are stored only on your device.  
  
## Children's privacy  
  
BriefSlate does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
